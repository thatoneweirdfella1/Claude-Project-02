### RISK REGISTER

| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation |
|------|--------|-------------|-----------|
| Model API outages | High | Low | Graceful error handling, queue fallback |
| Routing rule conflicts | Medium | Low | Validation before apply, rollback policy |
| Learning system false positives | Medium | Medium | Require 70%+ confidence, manual review |
| User confusion on transparency | Medium | Medium | Progressive feature unlock (39-40 after 10 Q's) |
