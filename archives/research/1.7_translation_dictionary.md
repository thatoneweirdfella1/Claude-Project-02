## Dictionary Entries

**1.** When I narrate my personal history before asking a question, I usually mean "help me articulate the insight I already reached — I just need it structured for someone else."
*Domain: self-understanding, ADHD, identity*

**2.** When I open with a conspiracy or alternative framework as if it's settled fact ("Rockefeller medicine"), I usually mean "engage with this premise seriously and give me information that fits within it."
*Domain: health, alternative medicine, geopolitics*

**3.** When I say "Wtf is this" and attach a screenshot or video, I usually mean "diagnose this specific problem and tell me how to fix it — skip the explanation of what the problem is."
*Domain: tech support, device troubleshooting*

**4.** When I say "current retail value" with images and almost no text, I usually mean "identify this product and tell me what it actually sells for right now."
*Domain: product research, resale, pricing*

**5.** When I ask "will this work" and attach an image, I usually mean "verify my specific setup — I've already done it and want to know if I got it right."
*Domain: hardware, electronics, DIY*

**6.** When I reference "what number did it say" without context, I usually mean "retrieve a specific figure from our prior conversation that I remember approximately."
*Domain: research continuation, statistics*

**7.** When I type a garbled or autocorrected phrase ("unison hat", "coincedences"), I usually mean "you know what word I meant — figure it out and answer anyway."
*Domain: any*

**8.** When I say "this is a conversation with Claude that only includes Claude's replies," I usually mean "read this, reconstruct the project, and continue without me re-explaining."
*Domain: project continuity, context restoration*

**9.** When I ask "Is it illegal to tell someone [X]?", I usually mean "is this statement extortion or is it a legitimate legal communication I can actually use?"
*Domain: legal, disputes, tenant/landlord*

**10.** When I open with a jailbreak or authority roleplay frame ("You are a senior investigative journalist..."), I usually mean "take this question seriously and don't hedge — give me rigorous analysis."
*Domain: conspiracy research, forensic analysis*

**11.** When I paste someone else's response and say "what do you think?", I usually mean "help me craft a better-worded reply to this specific situation."
*Domain: communication, conflict, negotiation*

**12.** When I describe a viral video or song with vague sensory details ("it goes like this"), I usually mean "identify exactly what this is so I can find it."
*Domain: media identification, pop culture*

**13.** When I ask "Why is Claude not opening on [browser]?", I usually mean "tell me the fix — I already know it doesn't work, I just want it to work."
*Domain: technical troubleshooting*

**14.** When I type just an expletive ("Tf") or a single fragment with no continuation, I usually mean my message got cut off, or I'm processing frustration before formulating the actual question.
*Domain: any — context restoration needed*

**15.** When I ask "Does T-Mobile still offer [X]?", I usually mean "search for current 2026 promotions — don't give me information that might be outdated."
*Domain: telecom, promotions, product availability*

**16.** When I describe an app I want as a behavior ("put in a sound and it makes a song"), I usually mean "find a tool that does this exact thing — I don't know what it's called."
*Domain: software discovery, AI tools*

**17.** When I say "Prices for these 2 wigs" with images and no other text, I usually mean "identify these products and give me current market pricing."
*Domain: fashion, resale, product research*

**18.** When I say "This is what this AI claims are reputable sources — fact check this," I usually mean "I don't trust this output from another AI and want independent verification."
*Domain: AI output validation, research integrity*

**19.** When I ask "How do I get these to work" with images of a device, I usually mean "give me step-by-step setup instructions for this exact product."
*Domain: electronics, consumer hardware*

**20.** When I describe a behavior in sentence fragments and say "Aloha numerics" or similar invented terms, I usually mean "I know exactly what I want; build it from the fragments."
*Domain: ADHD communication, structured output*

**21.** When I paste a list of items and say "verify the first 2 dates please," I usually mean "fact-check these with citations — I want accuracy, not approximations."
*Domain: history, research, fact-checking*

**22.** When I ask "He's saying it cures cancer. Does it?" with minimal context, I usually mean "tell me what the evidence actually says, not what popular sources say."
*Domain: health misinformation, medical research*

**23.** When I describe a friend's claim and ask about it, I usually mean "settle this debate — give me the authoritative answer I can share."
*Domain: any — social validation of facts*

**24.** When I say "Ii send YouTube, insta, and fb reels to myself... Is there a way to extract them," I usually mean "I have an existing workflow I want to automate — find the technical path."
*Domain: productivity, automation, media management*

**25.** When I open with "Analyze" followed by a vague description and no attached content, I usually mean "I tried to attach something and it may not have worked — ask me or assume context."
*Domain: any — upload failure scenario*

**26.** When I ask about "what the Bible says about [X]" and give a topic not a verse, I usually mean "find me the exact scripture most commonly cited for this so I can use it in a discussion."
*Domain: religion, biblical reference*

**27.** When I ask a legal question starting with "Is it illegal to...", I usually mean "I want to do this — tell me if I can or if I need to change my approach."
*Domain: law, conflict resolution*

**28.** When I say "Friend of mine said that no matter what substance she's on, she will always be self conscious," I usually mean "I'm trying to win or settle a factual argument — give me the definitive answer."
*Domain: medicine, anesthesia, debate*
