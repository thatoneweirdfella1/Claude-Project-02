**The best route is Alexa + Google Sheets via a free tool called IFTTT** (If This Then That). Here's the basic idea:

You say something like **"Alexa, trigger Rudy owes twenty dollars"** and it automatically adds a row to a Google Sheet with the date, name, and amount. Same thing for payments - "Alexa, trigger Rudy paid ten dollars" and it logs it as a negative/payment.

Then your Google Sheet becomes your running ledger that handles the math automatically.

**To set it up:**
1. Download the **IFTTT app** (free)
2. Connect your Alexa account to it
3. Create an "Applet" - the trigger is you saying a phrase to Alexa, the action is adding a row to Google Sheets
4. You'd make two applets - one for money owed, one for payments

The phrase structure Alexa uses with IFTTT is always **"Alexa, trigger [your phrase]"** - so you'd say "Alexa, trigger Rudy owes twenty" and it logs it without you touching anything.

The Google Sheet can then total everything automatically with a simple formula so you always see the running balance.

The only small hurdle is the initial setup takes maybe 20-30 minutes the first time. But once it's done, it's completely hands-free forever.
