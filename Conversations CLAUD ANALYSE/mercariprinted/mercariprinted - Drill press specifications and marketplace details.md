**HUMAN:**
What is a swing


**ASSISTANT:**
 **SWING = How wide of a piece you can drill through the CENTER of**

It's measured as the distance from the center of the chuck to the support column, **multiplied by 2**.

## VISUAL EXPLANATION:

Imagine you've got a round piece of wood and you want to drill a hole right in the dead center:

- **10" swing** = you can drill the center of a 10" diameter circle (5" radius from chuck to column edge)
- **13" swing** = you can drill the center of a 13" diameter circle (6.5" radius)
- **15" swing** = you can drill the center of a 15" diameter circle (7.5" radius)

## WHY IT MATTERS:

If you're drilling a hole in the middle of a 14" wide board, you need AT LEAST a 14" swing. With a 10" swing, you physically can't reach the center - the column gets in the way.

## HOW TO MEASURE YOURS:

1. Measure from the **center of the chuck** to the **front edge of the support column** 
2. Multiply that number by 2
3. That's your swing

So if you measure 6.5" from chuck center to column = 13" swing  
If you measure 7.5" from chuck center to column = 15" swing

**Think of it like this:** The swing is the diameter of the biggest circle you could mount on the table and still drill through its exact center point.

- Acknowledging multiple factors can be true simultaneously
- Resisting oversimplification even when it's easier or more satisfying

**Later theologians COMBINED these separate figures into one "The Antichrist"** - but the original texts describe:
- Many antichrists (1 John) - theological deniers of Christ
- Man of lawlessness (2 Thessalonians) - future deceiver in the temple
- The beast (Revelation) - possibly Rome/Nero
- Various Old Testament prophecies about tyrants
