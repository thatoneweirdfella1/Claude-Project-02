**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The report's stated conclusion from the Club of Rome's own foreword: the Earth's interlocking resources probably cannot support present rates of economic and population growth much beyond the year 2100, if that long, even with advanced technology.

Source: Meadows et al., 1972, pp. 173–4.

**DOCUMENTED FACT** — The report's technology warning, verbatim from the text: technological optimism is the most common and the most dangerous reaction to our findings from the world model.
