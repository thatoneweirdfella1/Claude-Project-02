## Why Your Listings Aren't Showing in Groups

There are **3 main culprits**:

**1. Facebook has a hard cap on group sharing from Marketplace**
Facebook curates which groups appear in Marketplace's "Sell" posting flow and limits how many groups are available — 25 is the documented maximum shown during listing. You cannot force Marketplace to show every group you've joined beyond that cap. So if you joined 20+ groups, you may only be reaching a fraction of them even when it "says" it posted.

**2. The "shared successfully" confirmation is a known lie**
A common bug causes Facebook to say "Your listing has been shared to…" and it never actually appears in the Buy and Sell groups. It's been reported across Android especially.

**3. Facebook's algorithm may be silently suppressing your posts**
About 20% of posts going to Facebook Groups fail silently — Facebook's system can't tell users exactly what's wrong, so it just... doesn't post. Sales-type language in listings can trigger this, causing posts to get flagged or rerouted.

---

## How to Fix It — Step by Step

**Step 1 — Post to Marketplace FIRST, then share**
Publish the listing in Marketplace first, then use the listing's "Share" button to post it into groups. This shares the Marketplace entry rather than re-creating a new listing, which preserves photos and details and works more reliably.

**Step 2 — Don't share to all groups at once**
Stagger your posts — share to 3–5 groups, wait 10–20 minutes, then share to the next batch. Doing it all at once triggers Facebook's spam detection and silently kills the posts.

**Step 3 — Verify it actually posted**
After sharing, click on the actual group and check manually. Don't trust the confirmation message — it lies.

**Step 4 — Check if your account has a soft restriction**
On your phone app, go into one of the buy/sell groups. If you see a blue "Request Review" button, tap it. Users have reported Marketplace being unsuspended within seconds of doing this.

**Step 5 — Clear app cache if on Android**
Go to Settings → Apps → Facebook → Storage → Clear Cache. Corrupted cache causes the app to show stale confirmation messages while posts fail silently in the background.

**Step 6 — Post directly IN the group, not from Marketplace**
Go directly inside each buy/sell group and create a post there. This bypasses the Marketplace sharing system entirely and puts you in full control of where it lands.
