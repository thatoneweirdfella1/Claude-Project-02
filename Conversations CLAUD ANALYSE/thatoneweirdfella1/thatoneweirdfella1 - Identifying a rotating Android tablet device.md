Short answer: **don't bother.**

The screen on a TV like this is not a practical repair. The panel itself costs nearly as much as the whole unit, and that's before labor. TV panels are made as one integrated piece in a very complex process — you can't fix a crack, and replacing the panel runs close to the cost of just buying a new set.

The StanbyMe runs around $700-800 new. A replacement panel for a 27-inch specialty display like that would likely run $400-600 just for the part, assuming you can even source it — and LG doesn't make it easy to get proprietary parts for lifestyle line products.

The realistic options are:

**1. LG warranty/support** — if it's still under warranty and the damage qualifies, they may cover it. Cracked screens from physical damage are typically excluded though.

**2. Sell it for parts** — broken StanbyMe units sell on eBay. Someone out there needs the stand, the base, the electronics board. You could probably get $50-150 for it broken.

**3. Replace it** — look for a used or refurbished one. They pop up on Facebook Marketplace and eBay for $300-400.
