Here's what you actually want and why.

**The key insight for your use case:** A diaphragm pump with a pressure switch is *self-limiting by design*. When the showerhead restricts flow, pressure builds, the switch trips, pump stops. It cycles back on when pressure drops. It won't fight the restriction, it'll just regulate to it. And it's safe sitting idle indefinitely because diaphragm pumps don't degrade sitting dry.

---

**Top pick: SEAFLO 33-Series 110V/115V AC**

Pre-installed with a standard 115V wall plug, no wiring experience needed. Just plug it into a power outlet and connect the plumbing. That's the "power cord" box checked without needing to wire up a 12V power supply.

SEAFLO is the top OEM pump brand in the U.S. RV and marine industry, used by manufacturers like Forest River, Thor, Winnebago, Grand Design, and others. These get factory-installed into vehicles that sit for months between use. Idle tolerance is not a concern.

The 33-Series runs at 3.3 GPM and 45 PSI with a smart pressure switch. That pressure switch is exactly what you want for showerhead compatibility. If your showerhead is rated at say 2.0 GPM, the pump will hit pressure, cycle off, and regulate. It won't blow the head off.

The Tdrsuper runs 5.5 GPM at 75 PSI with a power plug, self-priming, for home kitchen, bathroom, RV, marine, and garden hose use. More pump than you need, but the pressure switch still governs it the same way.
