### WHAT THE RESEARCH SHOWS

**DOCUMENTED:**

U.S. beekeepers reported losing 62% of managed honeybee colonies from June 2024 to February 2025 — the highest single-winter loss on record. This was published in *Environment: Science and Policy for Sustainable Development* (Vol. 67, No. 6, 2025).

Honeybees promote over 35% of food crops and 75% of flowering plants — and they are in unprecedented decline.

On causation: neonicotinoids are neurotoxic insecticides that target nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, interfering with neurotransmission and causing cognitive impairment, immune suppression, and oxidative stress. Developmental toxicity manifests in larvae as retarded growth, reduced feeding, and increased death. Queen and drone reproduction are impaired, lowering colony viability.

Neonicotinoids exhibit toxicity that can be amplified by other agrochemicals, and they synergistically reinforce infectious agents such as Nosema ceranae — together producing colony collapse.

**On the regulatory response:**
In 2018, the EU banned all outdoor uses of clothianidin, imidacloprid, and thiamethoxam. In 2020, it withheld renewal of thiacloprid's approval. In 2025, Quebec banned nearly all insecticide-coated seeds. Vermont banned neonic-treated seeds in 2024. New York passed similar legislation in 2023.

---

### PARTIAL ANSWER

The science is not contested at the peer-reviewed level — neonicotinoids are a documented primary driver of colony collapse. What *is* actively contested is at the regulatory and industry level, where the multi-factor argument (mites, climate, disease) is used to dilute the policy response and prevent targeted bans. Field studies have sometimes failed to show population-level effects after laboratory studies showed toxicity — this gap between lab and field findings is the industry's primary defensive position and it has been effective in slowing US-level regulation despite state-level movement.

The pattern: the EU acted on the precautionary principle. The US is acting state by state, slowly, against industry resistance.
