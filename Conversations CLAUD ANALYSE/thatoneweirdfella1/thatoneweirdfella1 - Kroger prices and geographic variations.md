Yes, prices absolutely vary by location at Kroger. Here's what you need to know.

**Kroger confirmed it themselves.** Kroger stated directly on Twitter: "Our pricing varies based on many different factors including location, area, division, etc." They also said prices "will always differ from other stores, even between our stores, because our costs will never be the same."

**So a random Google result showing a Kroger price could be wrong for your store.** It might be pulling a price from a different region or a different Kroger division entirely.

**The best way to check YOUR Kroger's actual prices:**

1. Go to **Kroger.com** and set your store to your specific location before searching items. Prices shown will reflect that store.
2. Use the **Kroger app** — same thing, make sure your store is selected.
3. If you have a Kroger Plus card, prices shown logged in will reflect your store's deals too.

You're in the Deer Park area, so your store is likely one of the Houston division Krogers. Prices there will differ from what someone in Colorado or Ohio is seeing.
