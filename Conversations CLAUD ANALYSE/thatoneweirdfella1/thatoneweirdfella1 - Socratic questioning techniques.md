**HUMAN:**

**ASSISTANT:**
 Based on what you've got so far, here's the sequence I'd run:

**Where he's at:**
- "Better or worse than last month?"
- "Is it one thing or is it everything at once?"

**What's actually going on:**
- "What's the heaviest part right now?"
- "Is this something happening to you or something you're carrying in your head?"

**What he's doing with it:**
- "You talking to anybody about it or just sitting with it?"
- "What would 'better' even look like for you right now?"

The last one is the key Socratic move. Gets him to define his own target instead of you projecting one onto him.
