HeyGen. You upload a reference image or short video clip of a person, it generates a photorealistic talking avatar that lip-syncs to any script you give it. This is the closest to what you want. Kling AI. Video generation that accepts image references. You can upload one of EPOCH's stills and prompt it to animate the face with specific expressions and motion. Doesn't do lip-sync natively but the visual quality is cinematic.

Runway Gen-3. Similar to Kling. Upload image, describe motion, it generates video. Best for subtle face movements, eye blinks, slight head turns. Good for the idle state animations between lines.

Pika Labs. Fast, good with portraits, accepts image references.
