**ASSISTANT:**
 They called themselves the **Nationalsozialisten** (National Socialists), members of the **Nationalsozialistische Deutsche Arbeiterpartei** abbreviated as the **NSDAP** (National Socialist German Workers' Party).

Informally, members used **Parteigenosse** (party comrade) or just referred to themselves by rank and role within the party or military branches.

"Nazi" was actually a derogatory nickname coined by opponents, playing on the German name Ignatz and the first two syllables of "Nationalsozialist." Journalist and political opponent **Kurt Tucholsky** is often credited with popularizing it as a pejorative. The party itself never used it.
