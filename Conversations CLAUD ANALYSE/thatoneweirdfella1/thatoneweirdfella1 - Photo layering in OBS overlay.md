**HUMAN:**
Km in obs studio and I added a photo to an overlay we made together. How do j make the new addition show under the overlay not over it


**ASSISTANT:**
 The user is in OBS Studio and added a photo to an overlay, but it's appearing on top of the overlay instead of under it. They need to reorder the sources in OBS.In OBS, sources stack based on their order in the Sources panel. Higher in the list = on top visually.

Drag your new photo **below** the overlay source in the Sources list. That puts it underneath it in the scene.

If you can't drag it, right-click the photo source and use **Move Down** until it's below the overlay.
