**ASSISTANT:**
 Right-click on the desktop, hover over **View**, and you'll see options at the top:

- Large icons
- Medium icons
- Small icons

Click **Small icons** (or Medium if Small is too tiny). That's the fastest fix and has nothing to do with resolution or scaling.

If you want even more control, right-click desktop → **Display settings** → find **Scale** (under Scale & layout). It's probably set to 125% or 150%. Drop it to **100%** and your icons will shrink along with everything else — though that affects the whole display, not just icons.

The "can only scale up" message just means your monitor is already at native resolution, which is correct. Resolution isn't the problem here. Icon size and display scale are the two levers you actually want.
