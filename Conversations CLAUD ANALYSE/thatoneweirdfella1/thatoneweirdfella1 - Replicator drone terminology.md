**ASSISTANT:**
 The saying is:

**"The meek shall inherit the earth"**

It comes from the **Bible** — specifically **Matthew 5:5**, part of the **Sermon on the Mount**, where Jesus delivers the Beatitudes. It also echoes **Psalm 37:11**.

---

**What it means:**
- **Religious interpretation:** The humble, gentle, and non-violent will ultimately be blessed by God and receive His kingdom.
- **"Meek" doesn't mean weak** — in the original Greek (*praus*), it meant someone who has power but exercises **restraint and humility**. Think of a trained warhorse that is powerful but controlled.
- **Secular interpretation:** Those who don't seek power or dominance will ultimately prevail — patience and humility win in the long run.

---

It's often used **ironically** today, since in practice the opposite seems true — the bold and aggressive tend to accumulate power and resources. That tension is kind of the whole point of the saying.
