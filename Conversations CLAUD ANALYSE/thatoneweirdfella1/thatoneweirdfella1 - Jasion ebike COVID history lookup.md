**ASSISTANT:**
 Yes, straightforward swap.

The Jasion EB5 uses a standard ebike battery lock cylinder. The battery slides onto a downtube mount and locks in place with a keyed barrel lock. Those barrel locks are replaceable.

What you need to search: **"ebike battery lock cylinder replacement"** or **"ebike frame battery lock with keys"**

Most run $8-20 on Amazon. They come with two keys. The cylinder pops out of the battery mount bracket, new one presses in and locks in place. No wiring involved, it's purely mechanical.

One thing to confirm before you order: look at where the battery slides onto the frame and find the lock hole. Measure the diameter of the cylinder if you can. Most are 10mm or 12mm barrel locks but sizing it first saves a return trip.

**HUMAN:**
How do I get the old one out


**ASSISTANT:**
 Depends on the situation you're in right now.

**If the battery is unlocked and removed already:**
The lock cylinder is usually held in by a retaining clip or a small screw on the back side of the mount bracket. You press the clip in or remove the screw, then push the cylinder out from the front. Needle nose pliers or a small flathead.

**If the battery is locked ON the frame and you can't remove it:**
That's the harder situation. A few options:

A locksmith can pick or drill it in about five minutes. Probably $20-40 if you bring it to them versus a house call.

You can drill it yourself. Use a drill bit slightly smaller than the cylinder, drill straight into the keyhole, it destroys the pins and the cylinder turns free. Then the battery releases and you pull the cylinder out and replace it. Cheap but permanent obviously.

Some people use a bump key or pick set but that's more skill dependent.

**What I'd actually do in your situation:**
Look at the cylinder first. If it's just the key that's missing and the battery is already off the bike, the retaining clip method takes two minutes. If the battery is stuck on there, drill is the fastest zero-cost option.
