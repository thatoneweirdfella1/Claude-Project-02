 The PLU code chart. Let's look at what's actually true here versus what's implied.

**What's accurate:**

9xxxx = organic. That one's real and standardized.

4xxxx = conventional. Also real. Four digit codes starting with 4 are standard conventional produce.

**What's misleading or flat wrong:**

8xxxx = GMO. This one gets passed around constantly. The IFPS (International Federation for Produce Standards) reserved the 8 prefix for GMO use but it was **never mandated**. GMO producers are not required to use it and almost none do. So you cannot identify GMO produce by an 8 prefix in practice. The label exists on paper. Not in stores.

3xxxx = irradiated. Also a reserved prefix. Same problem. Optional, not required. Rarely used in practice.

6xxxx = precut. This one is actually just a size/format code. Not a warning. Just logistics.

**The bigger issue:**

PLU codes were designed for inventory and checkout. They were never built as a consumer disclosure system. Framing them as a secret decoder ring implies the information is hidden. It's not hidden. It's also not reliably complete.

**What the chart does well:**

Gets people reading labels and thinking about what they're buying. That part has value.

**What it gets wrong:**

Implies the system is more coded and consistent than it actually is.
