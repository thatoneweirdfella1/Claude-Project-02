# Plasma donation blood pressure requirements

*2026-04-24T22:31:56.193351Z*

---


**HUMAN:**
What's the standard blood pressure amount that your pressure has to be to donate plasma. Something over something. What are those numbers


**ASSISTANT:**
 The standard requirement is **120/80 or below** on the systolic (top number), with the diastolic (bottom number) typically needing to be **100 or below**.

Most plasma donation centers require:

**Systolic: 90–180 mmHg**
**Diastolic: 60–100 mmHg**

So you need to fall within that range, not just be low. Too low gets you disqualified too.


**HUMAN:**
So 179/99 is the most?


**ASSISTANT:**
 Yeah basically. Most centers use **180/100** as the cutoff, so **179/99** would be the highest you could be and still pass.

Some centers are slightly stricter and cut off at **160/100**, so it can vary by location. Worth calling ahead if you're close to the line.
