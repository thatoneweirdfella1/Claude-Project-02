**Why the similarities feel so striking:**

- **Algorithmic content** — recommendation systems mean people watch similar content to others in their demographic, increasing overlap between life and media

- **Deepfake/video editing tools** — make it easy to fabricate or exaggerate visual "matches" after the fact
- **Search engines** — let people comb through vast media archives looking for matches retroactively

The short answer is: no technology enables actual future prediction. What exists is technology that makes coincidences more findable, more shareable, and more convincing-looking than ever before.
