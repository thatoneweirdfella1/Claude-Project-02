- **Marine/RV batteries** (100Ah+): 10-25A charger
- **Small batteries** (10-20Ah): 2-5A charger
- **Lithium batteries**: Follow manufacturer specs (often lower, 5-10A)

**Charging Speed vs. Battery Health:**
- Slower charging (lower amperage) = healthier for battery longevity
- Faster charging (higher amperage) = quicker but more heat/stress
- Never exceed manufacturer's maximum charge rate

**Best Practice Setup:**
- Use a smart/automatic charger (monitors and adjusts as needed)
- 13.8V output, 10-20A for most automotive applications
- Chargers with temperature compensation are better
- Keep battery at moderate temperature while charging
