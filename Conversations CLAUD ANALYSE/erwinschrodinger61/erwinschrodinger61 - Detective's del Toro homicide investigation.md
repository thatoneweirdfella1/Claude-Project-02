The files hit your desk like a thunderclap. Chief Briggs — red-faced, tie loosened, smelling like he hasn't slept since Tuesday — points one thick finger at the folders.

**"Double homicide. Two victims. No apparent connection. EXCEPT—"** he jabs the photo **"—both had ticket stubs. Del Toro films. ALL of them. Figure it out Malone. You're the only one I trust."**

He leaves. You crack your knuckles.

*Triplets,* you think. *Three tiny mouths. Benefits package. FOCUS.*

You open the first file. Victim one. Victim two. You pin them to the board and step back, narrowing your eyes.

---

**OKAY. CINEMATOGRAPHY. GUILLERMO DEL TORO. THINK.**

You start pacing. Something stirs... *not in your mind. Lower. Much lower.*

*Not yet,* you warn your body. *We are NOT there yet.*

You uncap a marker.

**"Light."** You write it on the board. Del Toro's films are ALL about the light. Warm amber in the safe spaces. Cold blue-green in the dangerous ones. *Pan's Labyrinth* — Ofelia's world, golden and amber. The real world? Desaturated. Cruel grey.

*Crimson Peak* — that house BLEEDS warm copper until it doesn't. Until the warmth means DEATH.

You freeze.

**WARMTH MEANS DEATH.**

You look at the crime scene photos. Both victims were found in — you check the notes — *warmly lit rooms.* Amber. Tungsten bulbs. Deliberate staging.

**"Oh no,"** you whisper.

Your stomach gurgles.

**"NO. Not NOW—"**

But the case is CRACKING open like an egg and your body KNOWS it and frankly your body doesn't CARE about your promotion—

You text the janitor preemptively:

> *"Hey Miguel. Might need you in about 4 minutes. Could go either way. Standby."*

Miguel replies instantly: 👍

---

You return to the board, slightly sweating, pressing on.

**DEL TORO'S EYES.** Every monster has beautiful eyes. The Pale Man. The Faun. The Amphibian Man. Del Toro said it himself — *the eyes are where the soul is.* His killers always have MAGNIFICENT eyes.

You pull victim one's autopsy report.

*Eyes. Removed post-mortem. Carefully. Professionally.*

Victim two.

*Same.*

**"SOMEONE IS COLLECTING THE EYES."**

Your bowels make a sound like a distant foghorn.

**"MIGUEL,"** you shout across the precinct.

**"ALREADY WALKING OVER,"** Miguel shouts back. A true professional.

---

You're FLYING now, marker squeaking across the whiteboard as things go south — *in multiple directions* — 

Del Toro's visual language — **THRESHOLDS.** Every film has a character crossing a threshold into another world. A door. A crack. A HIDDEN PASSAGE—

Both crime scenes — you flip back — both had *hidden rooms.* Rooms nobody knew about. Entrances that required—

You drop the marker.

You look at what's happening on the floor.

You look at the board.

You look at the floor again.

You text Miguel: *"Yeah it's bad today. Sorry man. The case is really something."*

Miguel: *"I can tell 😮"*

---

**YOU PICK THE MARKER BACK UP.**

Both victims had **keys** in their possession. Old-fashioned skeleton keys. Del Toro uses skeleton keys in *Pan's Labyrinth* — they unlock the world BENEATH. The real world. The hidden truth—

**THE KILLER IS USING DEL TORO'S FILMS AS A BLUEPRINT.**

A ritual. Warm light. Beautiful eyes taken as trophies. Hidden thresholds. A key to a world the victims didn't know they were entering.

The killer isn't just a fan.

**The killer THINKS they're making a film.**

You spin around, point at Detective Reyes across the room who has been pointedly staring at her computer screen for the last 90 seconds out of respect.

**"REYES. Pull every film school dropout within 50 miles who has a Del Toro obsession and access to—"**

**"Already on it,"** she says, not looking up. Also not looking down. 

**"Has anyone seen a third victim—"**

**"Malone,"** the Chief appears in the doorway, looking at the board, then at the floor, then at Miguel who is already halfway through his work with the quiet dignity of a true craftsman. The Chief looks back at the board. He nods slowly.

**"...You cracked it, didn't you."**

You straighten your tie. 

**"The kids are gonna have a college fund, Chief."**

*The case was yours.*

*It was always yours.*
