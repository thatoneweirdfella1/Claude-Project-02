## Why High Plasma Levels Matter — The Actual Mechanism

At low concentrations, Vitamin C is an **antioxidant** — it protects cells.

At very high IV concentrations, it **flips** and becomes a **pro-oxidant.**

- At high doses it generates **hydrogen peroxide** in the fluid surrounding cells
- Normal healthy cells have an enzyme called **catalase** that neutralizes hydrogen peroxide immediately — they're fine

The same substance that protects healthy cells **selectively destroys** cancer cells at high enough concentration. That's the mechanism. That's why dose is everything.
