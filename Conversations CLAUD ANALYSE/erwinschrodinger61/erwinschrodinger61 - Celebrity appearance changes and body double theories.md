## **9. WHERE PEOPLE GO WRONG**

5. **Baseline neglect** — Not accounting for the base rate (all humans change appearance; this is not unusual)

6. **Expert dismissal** — Geneticists, biologists, and medical professionals who explain why the claim is implausible are dismissed as "in on it"

7. **Unfalsifiability** — Any evidence against the claim is reinterpreted as evidence for it (biometric continuity = "clones are perfect," behavioral consistency = "controlled agents act normal")

8. **Anecdotal evidence treated as data** — Personal observations ("he seemed weird in that clip") treated as equivalent to documented evidence

9. **Photographic comparison fallacy** — Using heavily filtered, edited, or professionally produced images from different eras and claiming direct comparison proves discontinuity

10. **Necessity confusion** — Assuming something *could* be true if the technology existed, therefore it *is* true
