**ASSISTANT:**
 That button with the asterisk/snowflake-looking symbol is almost certainly the **screen rotation lock** button. On rugged tablets like your F8CT, it locks the display orientation so it stops rotating when you tilt the device.

Some variants of that button also function as a **Windows button** (home button) depending on the firmware, but rotation lock is the most common use for that symbol on rugged tablets.
