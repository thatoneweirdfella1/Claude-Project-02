**ASSISTANT:**
 The main mechanisms, in plain terms:

**Legal avoidance (the big ones)**

Borrowing against assets instead of selling them. If you own $500M in stock, you never sell it, so you never trigger capital gains tax. Instead you take out loans using the stock as collateral. Loans aren't income, so no tax. You live off the loan, repay it with more loans, and when you die your heirs get a "stepped-up basis" meaning the gain is erased entirely.

Buy-borrow-die is the name for that strategy and it's completely legal.

**Carried interest loophole.** Hedge fund and private equity managers get paid a share of profits (carried interest) that gets taxed at the long-term capital gains rate (20%) instead of ordinary income rates (up to 37%). Congress has closed this repeatedly in proposals and never actually done it.

**Pass-through entities and S-corps.** Business income gets routed through structures that minimize what counts as "wages" subject to self-employment and payroll taxes.

**Offshore accounts and foreign structures.** Legal when disclosed, frequently not disclosed. Panama Papers and Pandora Papers documented how extensively this happens even among people from countries with robust enforcement.

**Charitable foundations.** You donate appreciated stock to your own foundation, get a deduction at full market value, avoid the capital gains, and then your foundation funds things you control or care about anyway. The Gateses and Rockefellers perfected this.

**Cost segregation and depreciation.** Real estate investors depreciate properties aggressively on paper while the actual assets appreciate in value. Paper losses offset real income.

**The enforcement gap.** The IRS audits poor people at higher rates than rich ones because wage income is simple and automated, while complex returns with partnerships, trusts, and foreign entities require expensive specialist agents the IRS has been systematically defunded from hiring. ProPublica documented this extensively.

The short answer is: most of it is legal. The tax code was written with their lobbyists at the table.
