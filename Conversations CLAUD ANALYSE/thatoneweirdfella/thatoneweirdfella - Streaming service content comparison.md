Define aggregate


**ASSISTANT:**
 **Aggregate** means to collect or gather things from multiple different sources into one place.

In BeeTV's case, it pulls (aggregates) streaming links from Netflix, Prime, Disney+, etc. and presents them all in one app — it doesn't own or host any of the content itself, it just acts as a single access point for content that lives elsewhere.

A simple analogy: Google doesn't own the websites it links to — it just aggregates them into one search result page.
