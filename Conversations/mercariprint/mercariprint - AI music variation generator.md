**ASSISTANT:**
 What you're describing — uploading audio and prompting it to transform into variations — is basically **AI music remixing/stem manipulation**. Here are the best options right now:

---

## 🎵 Free (or Free Tier) AI Audio Tools

### **Suno** (suno.com)
- Free tier available
- You can describe a style and it generates music, but you **can't upload** your own audio to remix it
- Best for generating *new* tracks from prompts

### **Udio** (udio.com)
- Similar to Suno — prompt-based generation
- Free tier, but again no direct audio upload remixing

### **Adobe Podcast / Adobe Firefly Audio** *(emerging)*
- Adobe is building audio AI tools but remix-from-upload isn't fully there yet

---

## 🔥 Closest to What You Want

### **Musicfy** (musicfy.lol)
- Upload a song → change the style/vibe with a text prompt
- Has a free tier
- Lets you say things like *"make this more orchestral"* or *"add Irish folk instrumentation"*

### **Jammable** (jammable.com)
- Voice/style transfer on audio
- Free tier exists

### **Covers.ai / Voicify.ai**
- More focused on voice swapping, but can change the feel of a track

---

## 💡 Honest Reality Check

Truly **prompt-driven audio remixing** (like img2img but for audio) is still pretty early-stage and the free tiers are limited. The closest real workflow right now would be:

1. **Musicfy or Jammable** → upload the track, describe the vibe you want
2. If you want more control → **Suno** to *regenerate* in a specific style using the original as inspiration

What kind of variation are you going for — more instruments, different energy, fuller production? That might help narrow down the best tool.
