**CoT (Chain of Thought)**
You trigger this just by how you ask. No special setup needed.

> ❌ "What's 15% tip on $47?"
> ✅ "What's 15% tip on $47? Think through it step by step."

That's it. The phrase "step by step" (or "walk me through it," "show your reasoning") is the trigger. You can use it anytime, on any question.

---

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**
In my case, this means telling me to **search the web** instead of relying on my training data.

> ❌ "What's the latest on tariffs?"
> ✅ "Search the web and tell me the latest on tariffs."

I'll pull real sources and base my answer on those rather than what I already know — which may be outdated.

> "Search the web for the latest info on this, then walk me through your reasoning step by step."

That hits both at once. Most people don't know about either, so just knowing to ask for them already puts you ahead.
