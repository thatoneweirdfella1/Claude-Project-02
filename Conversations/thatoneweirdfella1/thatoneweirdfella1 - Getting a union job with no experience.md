Memory foam will grow mold fast, it holds moisture, and it's not designed to filter anything. It's also probably adding stuff to the water rather than removing it.

**Cheap effective filter layers in order from first to last:**

1. **Sand** - coarse on top, fine below. Catches sediment and bugs. Free if you can find it
2. **Gravel/small rocks** - pre-filter before the sand. Catches big debris
3. **Activated charcoal/biochar** - removes odors and some contaminants. You can make biochar by burning wood and quenching it. Free essentially
4. **Cotton or tightly woven cloth** - final layer before collection

Stack those in a bucket with holes drilled in the bottom, layers in that order top to bottom, water drips through into a clean collection container.

**The brown color:**

Probably tannins from the roof material plus debris. Multi-layer filtration will clear most of it visually.

**Critical point though:**

Even well filtered rainwater collected off a roof is not safe to drink without boiling or chemical treatment. The roof itself has bacteria, bird droppings, oxidized material. Filter it yes but boil it before drinking. Every time.

**Mold prevention:**

Let the filter dry between uses if possible. Charcoal resists mold better than foam.
