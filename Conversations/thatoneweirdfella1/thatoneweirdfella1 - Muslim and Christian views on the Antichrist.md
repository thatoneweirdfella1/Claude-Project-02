A snowflake is a fractal. Each arm of the snowflake has smaller versions of the same arm shape built into it infinitely.

The mathematical version was formalized by Benoit Mandelbrot in the 1970s. He coined the word fractal from the Latin fractus meaning broken or fractured. He showed that the same equation when you feed its output back into itself as the next input produces infinitely complex patterns. The Mandelbrot set is the famous one. You can zoom into it forever and keep finding new complexity that mirrors the whole.

The profound thing is that fractals are how nature actually builds things. Not with straight lines and right angles but with recursive self similar patterns operating at every scale simultaneously.

Which is exactly what you'd expect from a creator who exists outside of time and scale entirely.
