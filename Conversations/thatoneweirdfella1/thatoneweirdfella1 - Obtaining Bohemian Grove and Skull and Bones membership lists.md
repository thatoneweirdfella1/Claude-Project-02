## BOHEMIAN GROVE — SOURCE AND AUTHENTICITY STATUS

**DOCUMENTED FACT**

The Bohemian Club is a real, private, all-male organization founded in 1872 in San Francisco. Its annual encampment at a 2,700-acre compound in Monte Rio, Sonoma County, California is confirmed by the club itself, by decades of journalism, and by US government records including Reagan Library memos. The club limits membership to approximately 2,700 men. Initiation fee is $25,000 with a 15-year wait list documented by multiple sources. The club has never published a membership list.

**SOURCE:** Bohemian Club public records, Wikipedia list of Bohemian Club members (compiled from independent journalistic research), Mother Jones investigation 1981 (first documented inside account).

---

**DOCUMENTED FACT — 2023 LEAK**

In February 2026, independent investigative journalist Daniel Boguslaw published what he describes as the full 2023 camp attendance list — approximately 2,200 names — via his Substack "Deeper States." The list was published in collaboration with nonprofit outlet More Perfect Union. One club member, contacted by the San Francisco Standard, confirmed the list is genuine. The Press Democrat independently confirmed the list's authenticity. The Bohemian Club's own spokesperson Sam Singer — himself named on the list — stated the club "does not disclose nor confirm the names of members and guests" but did not deny the list's accuracy.

**SOURCE:** Boguslaw, Daniel. "EXCLUSIVE: I obtained the attendance list for the Bohemian Grove." Deeper States (Substack), February 24, 2026. danboguslaw.substack.com — PDF of full list attached and publicly downloadable. San Francisco Standard, February 25, 2026. Press Democrat, March 6, 2026.

**AUTHENTICITY TIER:** DOCUMENTED FACT that the list was published and confirmed by at least two independent outlets and one named club member. The list itself is publicly available as a PDF download from Boguslaw's Substack.

## SKULL AND BONES — SOURCE AND AUTHENTICITY STATUS

**DOCUMENTED FACT**

Skull and Bones (also known as The Order, Order 322 or The Brotherhood of Death) is an undergraduate senior secret society at Yale University founded in December 1832. It annually selects fifteen junior class members to join. Until 1971, the organization published annual membership rosters kept at Yale's library. There are no official rosters published after 1982 and membership for later years is often speculative.

**SOURCE:** Wikipedia. Skull and Bones. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skull_and_Bones. Yale University Library digital archives. Britannica.

---

**DOCUMENTED FACT — CONFIRMED HIGH-OFFICE MEMBERS**

The following are confirmed from published rosters (pre-1982) and independent verification:

- **Alphonso Taft** (1833) — US Attorney General 1876, Secretary of War 1876, co-founder of Skull and Bones
- **William Howard Taft** (1878) — 27th President of the United States, Chief Justice of the Supreme Court. Only person to hold both offices.
- **Henry Stimson** (1888) — Secretary of War under FDR during World War II, Secretary of State under Hoover
- **Averell Harriman** (1913) — Governor of New York, Ambassador to USSR and UK, key architect of postwar policy
- **Prescott Bush** (1917) — US Senator Connecticut; father of George H.W. Bush
- **Henry Luce** (1920) — founder of Time, Life, Fortune, and Sports Illustrated
- **McGeorge Bundy** (1940) — National Security Advisor under Kennedy and Johnson
- **William P. Bundy** (1939) — State Department liaison for Bay of Pigs invasion
- **William F. Buckley Jr.** (1950) — founder National Review, former CIA officer
- **William Henry Draper III** (1950) — Chair of UN Development Programme and Export-Import Bank
- **George H.W. Bush** (1948) — 41st President, 11th Director of Central Intelligence
- **John Forbes Kerry** (1966) — US Secretary of State 2013-2017, 2004 Democratic presidential nominee
- **George W. Bush** (1968) — 43rd President of the United States

**SOURCE:** Wikipedia. List of Skull and Bones members. en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Skull_and_Bones_members — sourced from Yale published rosters, academic records, and independently verified biographies. Scribd archived membership list (1833–1983) sourced from Yale library records.
