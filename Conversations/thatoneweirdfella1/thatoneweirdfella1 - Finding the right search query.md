**ASSISTANT:**
 There's no search query for that — it's a research and reasoning problem. Here's how people actually find it:

**Find the gap, not the idea.**

1. **Reddit/forums** — Search subreddits for "is there an app that..." or "why doesn't X exist." People literally describe the thing they wish existed.

2. **App store reviews** — Look at 1-3 star reviews of existing apps. The complaints are a roadmap of unmet needs.

3. **"Hire me to do X" posts** — Upwork, Fiverr, Facebook groups. If people are paying humans to do something repetitive, it can probably be an app.

4. **Your own friction** — What did you do this week that felt stupid or slow? That's usually worth more than any research.

5. **Exploding Topics / Google Trends** — Find things growing fast that don't have good tooling yet.

The demand signal you want is: *people already trying to solve this badly.* That's your proof of demand. You're not inventing a need, you're finding one that exists and isn't served well.
