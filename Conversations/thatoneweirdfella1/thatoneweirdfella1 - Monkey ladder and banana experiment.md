**ASSISTANT:**
 This is the actual documented study. Published in Science. Two thousand thirteen. University of St Andrews.

---

Researchers were working with wild vervet monkeys in South Africa. They set up a test with two food options. Corn dyed pink. Corn dyed blue. One group was conditioned to avoid the pink corn. It was made to taste bitter. Another group was conditioned to avoid the blue corn.

After the conditioning phase the monkeys consistently ate only the color their group had learned was safe. Even when untreated food of both colors was available later. The preference held.

Then they waited.

New infants were born into the groups. These infants had never experienced the original conditioning. They had no direct reason to avoid either color. But they watched their mothers. They watched the group. Every infant adopted the maternal preference. Every single one. Without exception. Without ever tasting the bitter version themselves.

Then the migration happened.

Male vervets naturally migrate between groups when they reach maturity. Several males moved from groups that ate pink corn into groups that ate blue corn and vice versa. These males had established personal preferences from their birth group. Real preferences. Learned through direct experience.

Within a short time the migrating males switched. They abandoned their own preference and matched the new group norm. They conformed even though their original preference was based on actual direct experience and the new norm was just what everyone around them was doing.

The researchers then checked overlap territories where males would already be familiar with the local food options. Conformity still happened.

No punishment. No hose. No beating. Just the pull of the group norm operating on its own.

The conclusion published in Science was that social learning in wild primates is a more potent force than previously recognized. The effect was strong enough to override personal learned preferences in adults and shape behavior in infants who had zero direct experience with the original reason for the norm.

The rule outlived the reason. Without any enforcement mechanism at all.
