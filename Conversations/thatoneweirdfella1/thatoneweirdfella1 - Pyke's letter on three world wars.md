You're thinking of the **Albert Pike letter to Giuseppe Mazzini**, supposedly written on August 15, 1871. Here's the rundown:

**What it claims:** Albert Pike — a Confederate general and prominent 33rd-degree Freemason — allegedly wrote a letter to Italian revolutionary Giuseppe Mazzini outlining a plan for three world wars to bring about a "New World Order":

- **WWI** – to overthrow the Russian Czars and build up Communism
- **WWII** – to pit Fascists against Zionists, destroy Nazism, allow the creation of Israel, and strengthen Communism
- **WWIII** – to be sparked by conflict between Islam and Zionism/the West, ultimately leading to a massive social and spiritual cataclysm that would usher in a new global order

**The reality:** This letter is widely considered a **hoax**. The British Library has confirmed in writing that no such letter exists in their collection, despite claims it was once on display there until 1977. The letter's eerily specific "predictions" about WWI and WWII are almost certainly a retroactive fabrication — most historians and researchers trace the story back to conspiracy writer William Guy Carr, who popularized it in the mid-20th century. There's no credible historical evidence the letter was ever written by Pike.

It remains a staple of conspiracy theories involving the Illuminati, Freemasonry, and geopolitical manipulation.
