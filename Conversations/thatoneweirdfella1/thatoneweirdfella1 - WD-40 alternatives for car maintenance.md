# WD-40 alternatives for car maintenance

*2026-06-08T01:38:31.599342Z*

---


**HUMAN:**
Can you use an alternative to wd40 when working on your car


**ASSISTANT:**
 Few options depending on what you need it to do.

**Loosening rusted bolts:** PB Blaster. Penetrates better than WD40 on seized hardware. Let it sit.

**Lubricating moving parts:** White lithium grease. Stays put longer. Good for hinges, cables, slides.

**Displacing moisture / light lube:** 3-in-1 oil. Cleaner than WD40, similar use cases.

**Protecting metal / corrosion prevention:** Fluid Film. Lanolin based. Lasts longer outdoors.

**High heat areas (exhaust, brakes):** Copper anti-seize. WD40 burns off. This doesn't.

**General lubrication:** Silicone spray. Doesn't attract dirt like WD40 does.
