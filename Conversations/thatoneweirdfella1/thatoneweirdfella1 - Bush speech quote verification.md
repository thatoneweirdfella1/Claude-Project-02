The exact quote from George W. Bush, delivered in Nashville, Tennessee on September 17, 2002, was:

*"There's an old saying in Tennessee — I know it's in Texas, probably in Tennessee — that says, 'Fool me once, shame on... shame on you.' Fool me — you can't get fooled again."*

He was attempting to say the old proverb *"Fool me once, shame on you; fool me twice, shame on me"* — but appeared to catch himself mid-sentence, likely not wanting to say "shame on me" as president, and ended up substituting in the Who lyric *"Won't Get Fooled Again"* instead.
