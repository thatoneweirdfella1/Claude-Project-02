# Sativa vs indica plant characteristics

*2026-05-30T22:15:42.043652Z*

---


**HUMAN:**
is sativa the short bushy plant and indica the taller thinner one


**ASSISTANT:**
 It's actually the opposite of what most people think, and also the opposite of what you said.

Indica is the short, bushy, wide-leaf plant. Sativa is the tall, thin, narrow-leaf plant.

The common confusion goes even deeper though. The sativa/indica distinction as most people use it (indica = body high/sedating, sativa = head high/energizing) is largely a marketing framework, not botany. The actual psychoactive effects are driven by cannabinoid and terpene profiles, not the plant's physical structure. A "sativa" strain can be sedating. An "indica" can be energizing. Depends on the specific chemical makeup.

The physical difference is real. The effect labels are mostly dispensary shorthand.
