# Semen sample container storage

*2026-06-07T07:58:08.375179Z*

---


**HUMAN:**
Do they vacuum the air out of seman sample containers


**ASSISTANT:**
 No. Semen sample collection containers are just standard sterile sealed containers, basically small screw-top cups or jars. They're airtight enough to prevent contamination but not vacuum-sealed.

The priority is sterility and pH neutrality, not atmospheric pressure. A vacuum would actually be counterproductive since you'd have to break the seal to use it, which defeats the purpose.

Some specialized cryogenic storage vials used for long-term sperm banking have a more robust seal design, but again, not vacuum.

Just a clean, sealed cup. Pretty low-tech for such a high-stakes deposit.
