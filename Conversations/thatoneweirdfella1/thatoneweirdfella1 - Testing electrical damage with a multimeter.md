**What you CAN check with a multimeter:**

Set to AC voltage. One probe on a known ground (a cold water pipe, or the ground slot of an outlet). Other probe touch the metal chassis. If you read anything close to 120V, the chassis has full line voltage on it. Even 30-40V is a hard stop.

You can also check continuity between the chassis and the ground pin of the plug. Should read near zero ohms. If it reads open (OL), the ground wire inside is broken.
