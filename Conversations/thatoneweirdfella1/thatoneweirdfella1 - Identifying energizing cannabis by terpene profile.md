Good budtenders know their inventory by effect. They'll point you toward what actually delivers that in their current stock.

Here's the full picture of where it stands right now in Texas, which covers Deer Park directly since there's no separate city ordinance doing anything beyond state law.

**What already happened:**

Hemp vapes and e-cigs containing any cannabinoids got banned in September 2025 under Senate Bill 2024.

Then March 31, 2026 hit, and smokable hemp products including flower and pre-rolls became prohibited in Texas retail.

The loophole that got closed was THCA flower. Manufacturers were growing hemp high in THCA, which converts to psychoactive THC when you smoke it. The new rules now count ALL THC forms toward the 0.3% limit, so that workaround is dead.

**What's still legal:**

Edibles, tinctures, and similar products are still legal if you're 21 or older with a valid ID and the product contains no more than 0.3% total THC.

**The backstory on how messy this got:**

The legislature passed Senate Bill 3 which would have banned THC outright. Abbott vetoed it, citing an Arkansas law that got tied up in courts for two years. He called lawmakers back for special sessions. Two more attempts stalled. Then the executive order route kicked in instead.

On top of that, Congress slipped a federal hemp THC ban into the government shutdown deal, which Trump signed. That bans hemp products with more than 0.4 milligrams of THC total.

**Bottom line for you in Deer Park:** Anything smokable or vapeable with real THC is gone from legal shelves. Edibles and tinctures under 0.3% are still technically available but that's basically CBD-level product. The market got gutted.
