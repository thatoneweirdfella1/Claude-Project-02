**ASSISTANT:**
 Prices vary by quality and source, but rough ballpark for Texas:

**Reggie/mids (low quality):** $100 to $150 per ounce

**Mid-shelf (decent quality):** $150 to $250 per ounce

**Top shelf/exotic:** $250 to $350+ per ounce

Texas is a non-legal state so prices run higher than legal markets like Colorado or California. No dispensaries, so everything moves through the gray/black market. No price regulation, no consistency. What someone in Houston pays can differ a lot from someone in a smaller city with fewer connections.
