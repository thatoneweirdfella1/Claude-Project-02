**ASSISTANT:**
 Yes. It's called a **vocoder** effect. Sometimes more specifically a **ring modulator** when it has that mechanical, slightly metallic quality.

The specific Saw implementation is often described as a **pitch-shifted vocoder** with a touch of **bitcrushing** to give it that hollow, slightly degraded texture.

If you're recreating it in audio software the search terms that'll get you there fastest are:

**Vocoder + pitch shift down + slight reverb + low bitrate crush**

Some people also describe it as having a **telephone filter** layered in, which cuts the high and low frequencies and makes it sound like it's coming through a speaker rather than directly.
