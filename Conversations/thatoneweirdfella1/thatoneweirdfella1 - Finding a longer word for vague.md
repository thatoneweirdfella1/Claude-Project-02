*2026-05-10T04:13:39.223342Z*

**HUMAN:**
What's the word that means vague but is longer and starts w an a


**ASSISTANT:**
 **Ambiguous.**

If you want something even more specific, there's also **ambivalent** (torn between two things) or **abstruse** (hard to understand), but ambiguous is the classic "vague/unclear" one.
