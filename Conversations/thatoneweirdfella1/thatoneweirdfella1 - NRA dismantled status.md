But there was also a second message. Police alleged she also wrote "There's going to be a bomb in the Ocean Bank Convocation Center." When other students expressed concern she wrote "I made a dumb joke that should not have been made."

She faces a second degree felony charge carrying up to 15 years in prison.

So the full picture. The Netanyahu part was clearly a meme. The second message about a bomb is what made it legally complicated. The judge said to an objective person it wasn't obviously a joke.

Still. A meme reference plus frustrated venting in a student chat turning into a felony charge with 15 years on the table. That's a significant escalation. And the fact that it involved Netanyahu specifically is not irrelevant to how seriously it got treated.
