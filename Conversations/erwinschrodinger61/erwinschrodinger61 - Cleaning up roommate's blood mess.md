**ASSISTANT:**
 Quick practical answer about blood cleanup.Go with the **bleach** — it's the right tool for blood specifically because it breaks down the proteins and disinfects at the same time. The Dawn and Fabuloso will clean the visible stuff but won't fully sanitize dried/smeared blood.

Here's the process:

1. **Mix 1 part bleach to 9 parts cold water** — always cold, hot water sets blood stains
2. Apply to the whole 7-foot radius, not just visible spots
3. Let it sit 5–10 minutes
4. Wipe up with paper towels (toss them, don't reuse)
5. Rinse with clean cold water

A couple of important notes — since you walked through it barefoot, wash your feet thoroughly with soap and water now if you haven't. And if there's any chance of bloodborne pathogen exposure (open cuts on your feet, etc.), that's worth a call to a nurse line.
