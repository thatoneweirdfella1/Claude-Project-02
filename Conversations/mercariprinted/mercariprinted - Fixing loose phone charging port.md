## 🆓 FREE FIX FIRST — The Paper Shim Method

Someone on Instructables figured out you can cut a tiny strip of paper, fold it once to double its thickness, tape it for strength, and slide it inside the port — between the phone casing wall and the connector. When you plug your cable in, it presses against the paper and grips tighter.

**Steps:**
1. Cut a thin strip of paper (like, fingernail-width thin)
2. Fold it once so it's doubled up
3. Wrap a tiny piece of tape around it so it holds shape
4. Slide it gently into the port — along the *side* of the hole, not blocking it
5. Plug cable in — it should grip tighter now

Think of it like how a wobbly table leg gets fixed with a folded napkin underneath. Same concept. You're just filling the extra space.

---

## 💲 CHEAPEST PAID FIX — Magnetic Charging Adapter

This is probably your best long-term solution honestly. You plug a tiny magnetic tip into your port **once**, and then every cable you use just snaps onto it magnetically. You never stress the port again.

Search: **"magnetic USB-C charging adapter"** on Amazon or Facebook Marketplace. They run **$5–$12.**

The cable never tugs. Never falls. Never loosens the port further.

---

## 🔌 About the One-Side-Only Issue

That's almost certainly **lint/debris packed into one side of the port.** USB-C is reversible because it has contacts on both sides — if one side's contacts are blocked by pocket lint, it'll only work flipped one way.

**Fix:**
1. Power the phone off
2. Take a **wooden toothpick** (not metal — metal can short something)
3. Very gently scrape along the bottom of the port
4. You'll probably pull out a compressed lint brick that looks like it's been in there since the Clinton administration
5. Try both sides again

---

## 💡 One Side Note

Before anything else — try the toothpick lint clean first. That fix is so stupidly simple it resolves both problems for a lot of people.
