## **The "Skin in the Game" Effect**

When people pay for something — even a small amount — they psychologically assign it **value**. When it's free, the brain literally registers it as **worthless**.

This is documented in **behavioral economics**. A famous study by Dan Ariely showed that when a product dropped from **$0.01 to free**, demand went up — but so did **carelessness and entitlement**.

---

## A metaphor that nails it:

Imagine two gyms. One costs **$50/month**. One is **free**.

The $50 gym is packed with people **actually working out.**
The free gym has people **standing around on their phones.**

Why? Because if you paid, skipping feels like **burning money**. If it's free, skipping costs you **nothing mentally.**
