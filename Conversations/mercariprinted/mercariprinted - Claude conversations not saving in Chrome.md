Here's the straight answer — two separate problems, two separate causes:

---

**Problem 1: Conversations don't save**

Claude in Chrome is a **browsing agent** — it's built to *do tasks* in your browser, not to be a chat interface. It's designed for multi-step browser workflows, not back-and-forth conversation storage. Conversations from the Chrome extension **don't sync to your claude.ai chat history** the same way regular chats do. That's a known limitation, not a bug you can fix.

---

**Problem 2: Alt-tabbing kills the whole thing**

The side panel is bound to a specific tab ID. When you switch tabs, the panel closes and the Claude browser session is lost — requiring you to reopen the panel and reconnect. This is a design issue with how the extension handles tab switching.

**Are you using Chrome or Edge?** Because Chrome handles this more gracefully by keeping the panel visible when switching tabs, but Edge closes it — so if you're on Edge, that's your culprit right there.

---

**The fix / workaround:**

1. **Use actual Chrome** (not Edge, not Brave — they don't support it properly)
2. **Don't alt-tab away** — use Claude in Chrome's built-in tab grouping instead. You can drag tabs into Claude's designated tab group to let it view and interact with all grouped tabs at once, so you never *need* to leave the panel
3. **For saving conversations** — just use regular claude.ai in a normal tab. That saves everything automatically. Claude in Chrome is for *automation tasks*, not conversations
