## **What the Dead Sea Scrolls Say Could Still Exist Today**

According to these ancient texts (particularly the **Book of Giants**, **Book of Enoch fragments**, and **Book of Jubilees** found at Qumran), here's what they claim survived and remains active:

---

### **1. Disembodied Spirits of the Nephilim (Demon Spirits)**

This is the big one. According to the giants' physical bodies were destroyed in the flood, but their spiritual/angelic nature survived (1 Enoch 15:8–12; 16:1). Consequently, according to this myth, demons are the spirits of the giants.

**Translation:** The physical giants died. Their spirits did NOT. Those spirits became what we call demons.

---

### **2. The 10% Rule (Jubilees 10)**

Here's where it gets specific: God binds only 90 percent of the Watchers and destroys them, leaving 10 percent to be ruled by Mastema. Because the evil in humans is great, only 10 percent would be needed to corrupt and lead humans astray.

So according to Jubilees, God intentionally left 10% of these evil spirits operational under a chief named **Mastema** (basically a Satan-like figure).

---

### **3. Named Evil Entities Still Operating**

The Dead Sea Scrolls name specific beings that were believed to still be active:

| **Entity** | **Role According to Scrolls** |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| **Belial/Beliar** | Leader of darkness, opposite of God in the War Scroll |
| **Mastema** | Chief of remaining evil spirits, permitted by God to test humanity |
| **Lilith** | The Songs of the Sage (4Q510-511) contains the first clear usage of the Hebrew term *lilith* in relation to a supernatural creature - mentioned in exorcism texts |
| **Azazel** | Watcher who taught humans forbidden knowledge (weapons, cosmetics, magic) |

---

### **4. What They Allegedly Do**

According to the texts, these surviving spirits:
- Corrupt and lead humans astray
- Cause disease and affliction  
- Possess individuals
- Operate under divine allowance (not outside God's control)

The scrolls actually contain **exorcism prayers** (Songs of the Sage, 11Q11) specifically designed to ward off these entities, which suggests the Qumran community believed they were actively encountering them.

## **HOW These Entities Allegedly Influence Humans (Per the Texts)**

According to 1 Enoch, the Book of Jubilees, and related Dead Sea Scrolls material, here are the **specific mechanisms of influence:**

---

### **1. DIRECT ACTIONS (Physical/External)**

From 1 Enoch 15:11: "The spirits of the giants afflict, oppress, destroy, attack, do battle, and work destruction on the earth, and cause trouble."

Breaking that down:

| **Action** | **What It Means** |
|------------|-------------------|
| **Afflict** | Cause suffering, torment |
| **Oppress** | Weigh down, burden |
| **Destroy** | Ruin, bring to nothing |
| **Attack** | Assault (physical/spiritual) |
| **Do battle** | Wage war against people |
| **Cause trouble** | Create chaos, disorder |

---

### **2. LEADING ASTRAY (Mental/Spiritual)**

From Jubilees 10:1-3: "The unclean demons began to lead astray the children of the sons of Noah; and to make to err and destroy them... the demons which were leading astray and blinding and slaying his sons' sons."

Three-pronged attack:
1. **Lead astray** — Misdirect thinking, wrong path
2. **Blinding** — Prevent seeing truth
3. **Slaying** — Kill (physically or spiritually)

---

### **3. CORRUPTION & TEMPTATION**

Mastema (the chief demon) explains his purpose directly. He says: "these are for corruption and leading astray before my judgment, for great is the wickedness of the sons of men."

So their explicit function is:
- **Corruption** — Moral degradation
- **Leading astray** — Deception, wrong choices
- Exploiting human weakness ("great is the wickedness")

---

### **4. DISEASE**

This is important. God "commanded that we should teach Noah all their medicines; for He knew that they would not walk in uprightness, nor strive in righteousness. And we explained to Noah all the medicines of their diseases, together with their seductions, how he might heal them with herbs of the earth."

The texts explicitly link demons to **diseases that require medicines/herbs** to cure. This isn't metaphorical — they believed demons caused physical illness.

---

### **5. POSSESSION**

Demons were thought to be the causes of various individual and social ills, such as disease, natural disasters, false religion, and illicit knowledge. Demons also sometimes took possession of the human body, an aptitude that likely stemmed from their origins within fleshly (giant) bodies.

Because they once had physical bodies (as Nephilim), they still crave physical form and can inhabit human bodies.

---

### **6. IDOLATRY & FALSE WORSHIP**

"They will make to themselves high places and groves and graven images, and they will worship, each his own graven image, so as to go astray, and they will sacrifice their children to demons."

Demons allegedly:
- Inspire idol creation
- Accept worship through false gods
- Demand human sacrifice (especially children)

---

### **7. THEIR HUNGER**

Weirdly specific detail from 1 Enoch 15:11: "they take no food, but nevertheless hunger and thirst."

They're described as having **perpetual unsatisfied craving** — no rest, no fulfillment. This connects to the concept in Matthew 12:43 about unclean spirits wandering "waterless places seeking rest but finding none."
