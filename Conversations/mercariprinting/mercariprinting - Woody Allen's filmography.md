# Woody Allen's filmography

*2026-02-21T02:03:23.492122Z*

---


**HUMAN:**
Woody allen. What movies did he make


**ASSISTANT:**
 Here's a rundown of Woody Allen's most notable films across his career:

**Early Comedies**
- *Take the Money and Run* (1969)
- *Bananas* (1971)
- *Everything You Always Wanted to Know About Sex* (1972)
- *Sleeper* (1973)
- *Love and Death* (1975)

**Critical Peak / New York Era**
- *Annie Hall* (1977) – won Best Picture, Best Director Oscar
- *Interiors* (1978)
- *Manhattan* (1979)
- *Stardust Memories* (1980)
- *A Midsummer Night's Sex Comedy* (1982)
- *Zelig* (1983)
- *Broadway Danny Rose* (1984)
- *The Purple Rose of Cairo* (1985)
- *Hannah and Her Sisters* (1986) – won him another Oscar for screenplay
- *Radio Days* (1987)
- *Crimes and Misdemeanors* (1989)

**1990s**
- *Alice* (1990)
- *Husbands and Wives* (1992)
- *Manhattan Murder Mystery* (1993)
- *Bullets Over Broadway* (1994)
- *Mighty Aphrodite* (1995)
- *Everyone Says I Love You* (1996)
- *Deconstructing Harry* (1997)
- *Celebrity* (1998)
- *Sweet and Lowdown* (1999)

**2000s**
- *Small Time Crooks* (2000)
- *The Curse of the Jade Scorpion* (2001)
- *Hollywood Ending* (2002)
- *Anything Else* (2003)
- *Melinda and Melinda* (2004)
- *Match Point* (2005) – critically acclaimed return to form
- *Scoop* (2006)
- *Cassandra's Dream* (2007)
- *Vicky Cristina Barcelona* (2008) – Penélope Cruz won Oscar

**2010s**
- *You Will Meet a Tall Dark Stranger* (2010)
- *Midnight in Paris* (2011) – huge hit, won Oscar for screenplay
- *To Rome with Love* (2012)
- *Blue Jasmine* (2013) – Cate Blanchett won Oscar
- *Magic in the Moonlight* (2014)
- *Irrational Man* (2015)
- *Café Society* (2016)
- *Wonder Wheel* (2017)
- *A Rainy Day in New York* (2019)

**2020s**
- *Rifkin's Festival* (2020)
- *Coup de Chance* (2023) – his first French-language film

He's remarkably prolific — essentially one film per year for over 50 years. His strongest critical periods are generally considered the late 70s and the late 80s/early 90s. *Annie Hall* and *Crimes and Misdemeanors* are often cited as his masterpieces.
