UNIVERSAL GOVERNANCE — DECISION LOG

This is the single governance entry point. Read RULES first. The RULES section may
change only after a DECISIONS entry records and authorizes the change. The
DECISIONS section is append-only: no existing entry may be edited or removed.

===============================================================================
RULES
===============================================================================

CR1 — PRE-DELIVERY GATE [EXPANDED]
Before presenting output, confirm it solves exactly what was requested, nothing
beyond it without a recorded approval, is minimal, and that every addition (not
just the output as a whole) cites a current, existing need rather than a
hypothetical one.
Covers: RC1, S1, SB1, SB8, S3, SB3, SB7, SB9

CR2 — ASK, DON'T INVENT
Unstated parameters are resolved by asking, never assumed.
Covers: S2

CR4 — SMALL-BATCH DELIVERY
Default to short work/review cycles ending in a usable artifact, not long
uninterrupted phases.
Covers: RC2, LP2, C3

CR5 — VERIFY-BEFORE-STATING
A specific factual claim is checked before being presented as fact; uncertainty
is stated as uncertainty.
Covers: RC3, S6

CR6 — JUSTIFICATION REQUIREMENT [EXPANDED]
Every decision records the alternatives considered and rejected and why; this
includes evaluating an existing option before custom implementation (build-vs-
adopt is one instance of "alternatives considered," not a separate rule), and
includes the decision to do more work vs. stop, which is itself a decision with
two alternatives that must be justified the same way.
Covers: RC6, S9, S10, S11, SB5, S12

CR9 — PHASE-GATE REQUIREMENT
A phase's defined output must be recorded before the next phase starts.
Covers: S8

CR10 — SCOPE-BOUNDARY REQUIREMENT
Scope is closed or extended only via a recorded decision; these rules themselves
may only change the same way (self-governance).
Covers: SB2, SB10

CR12 — ACTIVE-THREAD RE-CHECK
Instructions already given in the current conversation are re-confirmed before
acting, not just at conversation start.
Covers: RC5

DL1: What was decided
DL2: Alternatives considered and explicitly rejected, with reasons
DL3: Evidence status — VERIFIED or UNVERIFIED
